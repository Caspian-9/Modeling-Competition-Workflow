### 5.4 问题三带有偏差变量的随机规划模型的建立与求解

#### 5.4.1确定决策变量

由题意易知本问中决策变量为上一问的所有决策变量：

$$\left\{x_{ij},Y_{ij},u_{ik}^{A},u_{ik}^{B},u_{ik}^{C},\text {tra}_{i,j,k}\right\}$$

#### 5.4.2确定优化目标

本问中需要规划尽量多地采购A类和尽量少地采购C类原材料，同时希望转运商的转运损耗率尽量少，故优化目标为：

$$\min Z_{3}=wei_{1}\frac {d_{A,i}^{-}}{d_{C,i}^{-}}+wei_{2}d_{tran,i}^{+}+wei_{3}d_{chu,i}^{+}$$

#### 5.4.3确定约束条件

（1）对于A、B、C类原材料，其满足如下约束：

$$\left\{\begin{array}{l}\sum _{j=1}^{l}x_{ij}Y_{ij}+d_{A,i}^{-}=Q\times use_{A},mat_{j}\in A\\ \sum _{j=1}^{l}x_{ij}Y_{ij}+d_{B,i}^{-}=Q\times use_{B},mat_{j}\in B,i=241,242\cdots ,264\\ \sum _{j=1}^{l}x_{ij}Y_{ij}+d_{C,i}^{-}=Q\times use_{C},mat_{j}\in C\end{array}\right.\tag{5-4-1}$$

（2）每周的所选择转运商转运损耗率之和满足：

$$\sum _{k=1}^{L}\sum _{j=1}^{l}\tan _{i,j,k}U_{i,k}-d_{\text {tran},i}^{+}=0,i=241,242\cdots ,264\tag{5-4-2}$$

（3）此时考虑随机变量损耗率，记 $Q_{A,i}^{\prime },Q_{B,i}^{\prime },Q_{C,i}^{\prime }$ 分别为A、B、C类型第i周末库存量， $rel_{A,i}$ $\text {rel}_{B,i}、$ $\text {rel}_{C,i}$ 为 量 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}Q_{A,i}^{\prime}=Q_{A,i-1}^{\prime}+\sum_{i}\sum_{j=1}^{I}x_{ij}rel_{A,i}-R_{A,i},m_{B}t_{j}\in A,j=1,2\cdots,l\\
Q_{B,i}^{\prime}=Q_{B,i-1}^{\prime}+\sum_{i}\sum_{j=1}^{l}x_{ij}rel_{B,i}-R_{B,i},m_{j}\in B,j=1,2\cdots,l\\
Q_{Ci}^{\prime}=Q_{Ci-1}^{\prime}+\sum_{i}\sum_{i=1}^{l}x_{ij}r_{el}-R_{Ci,}m_{Ci}\in C,j=1,2\cdots,l\end{array}\right.$ 

$$(5-4-3)$$

$$\left\{\begin{array}{l}\text {rel}_{A,i}=\sum _{k=1}^{L}u_{ik}^{A}\left(1-U_{ik}\right)\\ \text {rel}_{B,i}=\sum _{k=1}^{L}u_{ik}^{B}\left(1-U_{ik}\right)\\ \text {rel}_{C,i}=\sum _{k=1}^{L}u_{ik}^{C}\left(1-U_{ik}\right)\end{array}\right.\tag{5-4-4}$$

$记c_{tran}$ 为单位转运与仓储成本，则转运与仓储成本为：

$$c_{\text {tran}}\left(\sum _{j=1}^{l}x_{ij}Y_{ij}+Q_{A,i}^{\prime }+Q_{B,i}^{\prime }+Q_{C,i}^{\prime }\right)-d_{chu,i}^{+}=0\tag{5-4-5}$$

则需求量为：

$$\left\{\begin{array}{l}R_{A,i}=E(A)+Q_{A}^{\prime }-Q_{A,i-1}^{\prime }\\ R_{B,i}=E(B)+Q_{B}^{\prime }-Q_{B,i-1}^{\prime },i=241,242\cdots ,264\\ R_{C,i}=E(C)+Q_{C}^{\prime }-Q_{C,i-1}^{\prime }\end{array}\right.\tag{5-4-6}$$

（4）任一周的库存量保持多于两周生产的原材料量：

$\frac {Q_{A,i}^{\prime }}{use_{A}}+\frac {Q_{B,i}^{\prime }}{use_{B}}+\frac {Q_{C,i}^{\prime }}{use_{C}}\geq 2Q,$ $i=241,242\cdots ,264$  (5-4-7)

（5）任一周的库存量为自然数：

$$Q_{A,i}^{\prime },Q_{B,i}^{\prime },Q_{C,i}^{\prime }\in N,i=241,242\cdots ,264\quad (5-4-8)$$

（6）对于转运的原材料量同样有：

$$u_{ik}^{A},u_{ik}^{B},u_{ik}^{C}\geq 0,i=241,242\cdots ,264,k=1,2\cdots ,L$$

（7）对于每一家转运商有每周运输量不超过6000立方米／周的约束，即

$$u_{ik}^{A}+u_{ik}^{B}+u_{ik}^{C}\leq 6000,i=241,242\cdots ,264,k=1,2\cdots ,L$$

（8）一家供应商每周供应的原材料尽量由一家转运商运输，需要明确的是，如果本文所选的供应商的供货量大于6000立方米／周，那么一家转运商是不够的。所以约束条件书写如下：

$$\sum _{k=1}^{L}x_{ij}tra_{i,j,k}=\left[\frac {Y_{ij}}{6000}\right]_{取整数}+1,i=241,242\cdots ,264,j=1,2\cdots ,l$$

（9）每周的正负偏差量应为正整数，即

$$d_{A,i}^{-},d_{B,i}^{-},d_{C,i}^{-},d_{\text {tran},i}^{+}\geq 0,i=241,242\cdots ,264\tag{5-4-9}$$

综上，问题三带有偏差变量的随机规划模型为：

$$\min Z_{3}=wei_{1}\frac {d_{A,i}^{-}}{d_{C,i}^{-}}+wei_{2}d_{tran,i}^{+}+wei_{3}d_{chu,i}^{+}$$

$$s.t.式(5-3-10,11,\cdots ,14)\quad 、式(5-4-3,4\cdots ,9)$$
